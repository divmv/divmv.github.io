Selected projects

My favorite work is the kind where software has to meet hardware, timing, noise, and the occasional loose wire.

01 / FPGA · 2025–26

PrimeKart: a prime-number computer

I led the architecture and integration for a Nexys A7 system that tests numbers for primality. The design uses a hardware Sieve of Eratosthenes, 1-bit BRAM, dual FIFO IP, and DDR2 through Xilinx MIG. A keypad sets the test; VGA shows the result.

PARMCO motor control

A phone app talks to a Raspberry Pi over Bluetooth to control a motor. Choose direction and speed, set a target RPM, and see live feedback. I built the control flow and MOSFET power stage.
